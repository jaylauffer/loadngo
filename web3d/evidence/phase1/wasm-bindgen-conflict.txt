## 5. Pinning 0.2.106 instead (already in the lock) fails at the bindings step: the schemas differ.
wasm-bindgen-shared 0.2.106: pub const SCHEMA_VERSION: &str = "0.2.106";
wasm-bindgen-shared 0.2.130: pub const SCHEMA_VERSION: &str = "0.2.130";

## Outcome: no dependency at all. js/gl.js is imported over the raw wasm C ABI (module web3d_gl),
## so the crate stays a workspace member, CI checks it on every platform, and build.sh needs no CLI.
